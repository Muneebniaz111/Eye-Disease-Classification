Cataract

Clouding of the eye's natural lens that can blur or dim vision. Often age-related and commonly treatable with surgery.

Diabetic Retinopathy

Damage to the retina's blood vessels linked to diabetes. Early detection helps guide treatment and prevent vision loss.

A group of conditions that damage the optic nerve, often tied to elevated eye pressure. Early care can slow progression.
